- seeds: 390001–390006
- six-seed leave-one-seed-out protocol
- five source seeds train the predictor

- ticks per seed: 500
- history lengths: 2, 3, 5

## Interpretation

GENESIS-2.23 does not establish cross-seed predictive generalization.

The pooled predictor fails the primary criterion in all 18 tested cases: it never beats the zero-change baseline. It beats the shuffled control in 11/18 cases, showing that some order-sensitive structure remains, but this is not sufficient evidence of useful predictive transfer.

The larger six-seed screen therefore strengthens the negative conclusion already reached by GENESIS-2.22.

## Research status

The current evidence supports the following distinction:

1. GENESIS-2.9 established a reproducible within-seed predictive signal in recent coherence innovations under the tested protocol.
2. GENESIS-2.10–2.19 did not identify a robust non-coherence representation that reconstructs that signal.
3. GENESIS-2.20–2.23 increasingly tested transfer and seed invariance.
4. The cross-seed family remains negative under the tested protocols.

No universe rule was changed. No prediction was fed back into the universe. No goals, rewards, agency, self-model, or endogenous learning were introduced.

## Next research direction

The next probe should not simply add more seeds or more raw observer dimensions.

The open question is whether the predictive structure can be represented in a way tied to the **underlying universe rule** rather than to seed-specific observer coordinates.

A future positive result should require:
- held-out improvement over zero-change,
- superiority to shuffled control,
- cross-seed validation,
- explicit protocol definition before evaluation,
- and preservation of the measurement-only invariants.
